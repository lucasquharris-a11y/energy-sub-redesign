Mechanism

How Tokens Are
Created & Redeemed

A transparent mint-and-burn process that maintains 1:1 backing and real-time market balance — similar to ETF creation.

01

Order Received

Mint and redeem orders arrive through our institutional portal or trading desk, creating net primary issuance for the day.

→
02

Supply Matched

Energy suppliers submit bids to substantiate from inventory — including price and capital tenor. Supply meets demand.

→
03

Tokens Issued

Buyer receives tokens at the pegged price. Assets are held by an independent custodian with full transparency in real time.

→
04

Trade or Redeem

Trade on-chain 24/7 with instant settlement or redeem at pegged price in cash. Supplier repurchases energy on cycle.
